Privacy Policy

Ultimate Car Parking 3D
Android package com.ultimateparking.carparking3d
Effective 6 November 2026

This policy describes how the Ultimate Car Parking 3D app (“the app”) handles information. The app is published by Ultimate Parking.

Information the app stores

The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or microphone audio.

Progress is saved only on your device, in the app’s private storage. That file can include:

  • coins, the car you have selected, and which cars you own
  • level results, including stars, best time, and how many times a level was completed
  • World Tour choices, including the selected country and unlocked countries
  • settings for music volume, sound volume, and steering sensitivity
  • the time the save was last written

This information stays on the device. The app does not send it to Ultimate Parking or to an analytics or advertising service.

Purchases

Cars are bought with coins earned inside the game. The app does not offer real-money purchases and does not process payment information.

Advertising and third parties

The app does not show ads and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

The app is a driving and parking game. It is not directed at collecting personal information from children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

How to delete your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes the local save file from the device. There is no online account to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated page will be published at this same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by opening an issue on the project repository: github.com/chaubeyprashant/ultimate_parking_3d.
