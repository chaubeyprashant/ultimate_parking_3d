# Asset research — batch 2: China, South Korea, Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia

Research date 2026-11-06. **Research only: nothing here has been downloaded.** Candidates come from Sketchfab's public search API (filters: downloadable, CC0 or CC BY, ≤120k faces). Licence, author, title and face count are the API's own values; links are `https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/<id>`. Nothing was typed from memory.

All candidates below are **CC BY 4.0** (attribution required; the in-game Credits screen is still to be built). The CC0 results were irrelevant (museum scans of statues).

**Excluded on purpose**
- **Real car brands (trademark):** Daewoo Lanos, Chevrolet Spark, the Honda Civic police car, BYD E6.
- **Petronas / Petron gas stations:** real brands.
- **Interiors, characters, food, statues and scans.**
- **Models over about 60k faces** unless noted. They would be too heavy for the A33; any that are kept are decimated or cut up.
