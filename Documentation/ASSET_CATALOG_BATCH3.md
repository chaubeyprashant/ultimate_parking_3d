# Asset research — batch 3: Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, France, Germany, Italy

Research date 2026-11-07. **Research only: nothing here has been downloaded.** Candidates come from Sketchfab's public search API (downloadable, CC0 or CC BY, ≤120k faces; 35 queries × 2 licence filters, 612 unique results, then filtered by keyword, face count 1.2k–90k and likes). Title, author-handle, licence and face counts are the API's values. Links: `https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/<id>`. The CC0 results were all museum scans (irrelevant). All candidates are **CC BY 4.0** — attribution goes in the Credits screen (still to be built) and ATTRIBUTION.md.

**Excluded on purpose:** Bajaj Pulsar motorbikes (brand), Vespa (brand), Eiffel Tower phones, statues/scans, interiors, characters, ruins/cathedral interiors, models over ~60k faces (unless cut up).
