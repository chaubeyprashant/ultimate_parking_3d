# Asset research — batch 6: South Africa, Egypt, Australia, New Zealand, Turkey

Research date 2026-11-07. **Research only: nothing here has been downloaded.** Source: Sketchfab public search API (downloadable, CC BY, ≤100–120k faces; two passes, about 80 queries, roughly 900 unique results, then keyword/face-count filter). Title, author handle and face counts are the API's own values. Link form: `https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/<id>`. All candidates are **CC BY 4.0**.

**Excluded on purpose:** weapons and military vehicles, trains, characters, animals, a casino model that mimics a real venue, "bazaar" results that are actually a fantasy/steampunk game-art set, models over about 60k faces unless cut up.

## Honest summary of coverage — this batch is the weakest so far

| Country | Coverage | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Egypt | Fair | Temples, ruins, a pyramid, a stone-mason's shop. Fantasy-flavoured; no modern Cairo street. |
| Australia | Fair-thin | Sydney apartment blocks, Sydney Opera House (62k, heavy), Uluru, and generic suburban/beach houses (not specifically Australian). |
| Turkey | Thin | Maiden's Tower (two versions), Galata Tower, a city-walls piece. No houses, no bazaar, no mosque (the Stylized Mosque from batch 3 can be reused). |
| South Africa | **None usable** | Only a caretaker shack and a Cape Town terrain. No township, no street set. |
| New Zealand | **None specific** | Only generic farmhouses and a lighthouse. Nothing identifiably New Zealand. |

Recommendation: approve **Egypt, Australia and Turkey** with the small sets below. For **South Africa and New Zealand**, the honest options are (a) a different asset source (for example CC0 packs from Poly Haven, Quaternius or Kenney, which I would research separately and ask about before any download), or (b) build them from generic stand-ins already in the project and label the arenas as such. I will not present generic houses as South African or New Zealand.
