# Asset research — batch 4: Spain, Russia, Netherlands, Switzerland, Greece

Research date 2026-11-07. **Research only: nothing here has been downloaded.** Source: Sketchfab public search API (downloadable, CC BY or CC0, ≤120k faces; about 100 queries over two passes, 656 unique results in pass one). Title, author handle, licence and face counts are the API's own values. Link form: `https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/<id>`. All candidates are **CC BY 4.0**; the few CC0 hits were museum scans.

**Excluded on purpose:** weapons (Swiss rifles, bayonets), watches, knives, props and characters; Camp Nou is kept optional only (a real club's stadium name); models over about 60k faces unless cut up; the "Apocalypse | Dirty" house (post-apocalyptic look).
